02 / Hard Skills

Practical Craft

Workshop: Presentation Deck Design. Flip through a preview below. Use the arrows, swipe, or press ← →. Press B to blank the screen the way a presenter would.

Workshop · Deck Design

Decks that decide.

Three sessions, one clicker, and no wall-of-text slides.

Rule 01

Lead with the answer.

Put the conclusion in the title, then back it with three points at most.

Rule 02

One slide, one message.

If a slide needs two titles, it needs to be two slides.

1
Rule 03

Make charts speak.

Highlight the one bar that matters and label it directly. Grey out everything else.

Rule 04

Rehearse with the clicker.

Blank the screen (B), pause, and let the room look at you. Try the ▭ button on the remote.

Your turn

Bring a deck. Leave with a better one.
